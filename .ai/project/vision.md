# Project Vision

Durly is a native, free and open-source macOS menu bar application that helps MacBook owners preserve battery capacity over time.

## Problem

Users need understandable battery diagnostics and safe charging management, including on older Intel Macs.

## Desired outcome

A lightweight, offline tool that reports trustworthy telemetry, applies verified charge limits when supported, and makes limitations and recovery clear.

## Non-goals

No battery rejuvenation promises, deliberate deep discharge, cloud account, or unverified hardware control.
